Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle: Color Blast

Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Package ID: com.aistudio.colorflowmaster.kxmpzq

This Privacy Policy describes how Block Puzzle: Color Blast ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you play the mobile game on Android.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Block Puzzle: Color Blast does not require account registration and does not collect your name, phone number, or personal profile directly. Your game progress—including best score, coin balance, unlocked themes and block styles, daily reward streak, daily challenge progress, and preferences (sound, vibration, colorblind mode, privacy preference)—is stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising & Consent

Block Puzzle: Color Blast is a free, ad-supported game that displays banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads using Google AdMob and manages regional ad consent preferences via Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, app interaction events, and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads according to your consent choices.

  • When privacy options are required in your region, you can review or update your ad consent preference anytime inside Settings → Privacy Options.
  • Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

The app uses minimal permissions required for ads and gameplay feedback: INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (for Google AdMob), and VIBRATE (for optional haptic feedback). It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or external media files.

4. Children’s Privacy

Block Puzzle: Color Blast is a general-audience puzzle game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

5. Deleting Your Data

You can clear your saved game progress at any time using Settings → Reset Progress inside the app, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling Block Puzzle: Color Blast from your device.
